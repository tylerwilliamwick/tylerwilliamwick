“I build where maps, AI, and government operations meet.”

My work sits at the intersection of geospatial technology and civic platforms. In more than five years in Accela’s product organization — progressing from project management into associate product management and product management — I helped productize and expand the 2023 Rapid Damage Assessment offering, assessed 15 emerging Esri GeoAI capabilities for operational readiness, and directed ArcGIS Enterprise compatibility and upgrade planning across an integrated government customer base.

Beyond GIS and AI, I bring depth in 311/service-request commercialization, lifecycle and migration programs, and executive communication. I believe the PM’s job is to make the hard tradeoffs visible so the right people can make the right calls.
